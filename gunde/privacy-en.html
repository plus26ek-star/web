Privacy Policy

GUNDE? does not collect any personal data. Your thoughts and settings stay on your device and are never sent to anyone, including us.

1. Data we collect

GUNDE? (the "App"), provided by joydusoft ("we"), has no accounts or sign-in, and does not collect, store, or transmit your personal data.

2. Data stored only on your device

The following data is created as you use the App and is stored only on your device. We cannot see it.

  • The thoughts you write and when you wrote them
  • Whether the daily reminder is on, and its time
  • Whether you have seen the welcome screen

If iCloud Backup or computer backups are turned on, this data may be included in your device backup, which is governed by Apple and your backup settings.

3. Downloading daily prompts

To get new daily prompts, the App downloads a single public file from the internet (GitHub Pages). The App sends no information when doing so — none of your thoughts and no device identifiers. As with any internet connection, the server hosting the file may receive technical information such as your IP address, but we do not collect or use it. Every feature of the App works without an internet connection.

4. Notifications

The daily reminder is a local notification scheduled on your device. No device information or push token is sent anywhere. You can turn reminders off at any time in the App's Settings tab or in the iOS Settings app.

5. Sharing, processors, and tracking

Because we collect no personal data, we do not share it with or entrust it to third parties. The App uses no advertising, analytics, or tracking.

6. Deleting your data

  • Go to Settings → Delete All Records in the App to erase every thought.
  • You can also delete individual flows or thoughts from your records.
  • Deleting the App removes all data it stored on your device.

7. Contacting us by email

If you email us, we receive your email address and message in order to reply. We use them only to respond, and delete them once your inquiry is resolved, unless the law requires us to keep them longer.

8. Children

The App does not collect personal data from anyone, of any age.

9. Contact

  • Privacy officer: joydusoft
  • Email: amusingsoft@gmail.com

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the changes and the effective date on this page. If the App ever starts handling new kinds of data, this page will be updated before that App update is released.
