FAQ

Where are my thoughts stored?

Only on this device. Nothing is sent to a server, and the developer can't see it. The App only uses the internet to download new daily prompts and to open the Privacy Policy.

Can I move my records to a new device?

Yes — restoring a new device from an iCloud or computer backup brings your records along. The App has no account sync of its own.

I'm not getting the daily reminder.

Check that Settings → Daily Reminder is on in the App, and that notifications are allowed in the iOS Settings app → Notifications → A Thought. If you've already written that day, no reminder is sent.

My images aren't saving to Photos.

In the iOS Settings app → Apps → A Thought → Photos, allow Add Photos Only. The App only adds images; it never reads your photo library.

How do I delete my records?

Delete a single flow from the More menu on its screen, or everything at once in Settings → Delete All Records. Deleted records can't be restored.
